Decisions

  1. Trim's shadow: the piece fades and turns dashed in its own colour; or dashed in red; or the amber hover mark on the piece only.
  2. Trim and Extend cursor: the pick box (prompts say "Select…"); or keep the crosshair.
  3. Rotate with several objects: all of them; devices move round the base point, cameras and speakers also turn; or drawn objects only; or one at a time as today.
  4. Scale: factor typed, shadow shown while typing; or also drag to scale.
  5. Repeat hint: on the command line when a command ends; or the grey hint in the empty command box.
