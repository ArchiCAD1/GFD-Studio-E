SLD — show and draw it by substation, RMU and transformer (proposal mockup, 2026-11-18)

Example project: five substations, six RMUs, seven transformers, and one board on an authority LV supply (MDB-8). Each main board already names its RMU and its substation in Panels → Transformers; today the SLD draws neither the substation nor the RMU's ways, and always shows the whole network. Sheets print in black and white; the screen views tint the 11 kV lines as the app does today. All ratings are example values.

new or changed numbered point in the text

1 · Today every transformer, and the meter cabinet too, hangs from one 11 kV line under one utility symbol

Panels

1One line for everything. TX-1 … TX-7 all drop from the same 11 kV line. MDB-8 is on a DEWA LV supply, but its meter-cabinet line crosses the 11 kV line, so it looks fed from it too.

2RMUs are labels. RMU-1 … RMU-6 are boxes laid over that line. Their ring and tee-off ways are not drawn, nor is the ring from one RMU to the next.

3No substations. SS-1 … SS-5 are set on the transformers. The TCL summary, the earthing schematic and the BOQ group by them; the SLD does not.

4Always the whole network. The SLD page has no way to show one substation, RMU or transformer, and the panel tree lists MDB-1 … MDB-8 flat.

2 · Proposed — the supply arrangement, drawn by substation the whole network; the same drawing on screen and on sheets

1SS-1: one RMU (RRTT) → two transformers, bus coupler between MDB-1 and MDB-2 2SS-2: one RMU (RRT) → one transformer, circuit breaker tee-off with relay 3SS-3: two RMUs in one substation, each → one transformer 4SS-4: RRTT with one tee-off spare 5SS-5: normally open point (N/O) on the ring 6MDB-8: authority LV supply, off the 11 kV ring

Main boards are placed by substation, then by RMU. The ring runs from the grid source through each RMU's ring switches (R) in substation order; tee-offs (T) feed the transformers. Each named substation gets a dash-dot frame around its RMU(s) and transformers. Everything from the transformer's LV side down is drawn as today. A second grid source would start a second ring.

3 · Show one substation SLD ribbon → View → Show; the menu is the supply tree, so a substation, an RMU or a transformer can be picked

Show sits in the View group beside Fit. Its menu lists Whole network, then each substation with its RMUs and their transformers, the way they are connected. Each row shows the transformers and boards it holds. One click shows that part.

Rows: a substation shows its RMUs, transformers and main boards, with everything they feed; an RMU shows its transformers; a transformer shows its main board's tree. The ring, and anything outside the part, is drawn as short stubs with "from …" / "to …" labels.

Only the view changes. Calculations, checks, results and fault levels are those of the whole network. Fit fits the shown part. Export exports what is shown, titled e.g. "SLD — SS-1".

Boards on an authority LV supply are listed after the substations. "Selection's substation" shows the substation of the board picked on the drawing.

Optional, in the same step: Colour by → Transformer tints each transformer's branch on screen; sheets print black, as with Colour by → Supply.

7 · Decisions and steps

Decisions

  1. Substation frame — around the RMU(s) and transformers (recommended), or down to the main board's incomer.
  2. Ring — an open line through the RMUs with "from" / "to" labels (recommended), or a closed loop back to the source.
  3. Show menu — one supply tree, substation → RMU → transformer (recommended), or three separate lists.
  4. A board outside the shown part is picked (panel tree, Check, Find) — show its substation (recommended), or the whole network.
  5. Panel tree — add the Group switch, Boards stays the default (recommended), or leave the tree as it is.
  6. Substation details — one record shared with the DM-D-013 Substation area page (recommended), or a separate list.
  7. Order — steps 1 → 5 (recommended), or Show first.

Steps (one PR each, with tests)

  1. The supply arrangement on the SLD (section 2), plus two fixes: the RMU limit from its tee-offs, and Substation area → Fill from project by substation name (today one substation per RMU, so SS-3 becomes two).
  2. Show, the chip with ◀ ▶, and right-click Show … only (sections 3 and 4).
  3. The panel tree by substation (section 3).
  4. The Substations card and right-click Substation / RMU (section 5).
  5. One sheet per substation (section 6).
