What changes

  1. One row on every tab. The panel buttons stay while they all fit on the row beside the tabs (MDB, SMDB, MCC today). The rule is the window width, not a fixed number: a wide screen keeps buttons longer.
  2. When they do not fit, the row shows ◀ DB-83 ▶: the open panel, its floor and its place in the list (84 of 84). ◀ ▶ step through the list, as the ribbon's previous / next already do.
  3. "! 83 of 84 not filled" with Next not filled: jumps to the next panel whose schedule is still empty, so you work through them in order.
  4. The list opens below the panel name (click, or Ctrl/⌘ K). It has a search box (number, name or room), a "Not filled only" tick, and groups by floor from Building information. Without floors it groups by the panel each one is fed from, and if that is the same for all, it is one list. The ! marks stay. Keys: type to filter, arrows, Enter, Esc.
  5. Number order in the list: DB-1, DB-2 … DB-83 within each floor, instead of the supply order (DB-GF1, DB-41 … DB-83, DB-1 … DB-40 in your picture). ◀ ▶ follow the list.
  6. Space: with 84 DBs, the tabs and panel buttons take : about 5 more circuit rows at this width, about 7 on a window where the buttons take six rows (your picture). Tabs with few panels look as they do now.

Decisions for you

  • A. Many panels: the ◀ panel ▾ ▶ picker with the list (recommended), or one row of buttons that scrolls sideways with ◀ ▶ at its ends?
  • B. When to switch: when the buttons no longer fit on the row (recommended), or above a fixed count (say 12)?
  • C. Groups in the list: floor, else fed-from panel (recommended), or no groups?
  • D. Order: number order in the list and for ◀ ▶ (recommended), or keep the supply order?
  • E. The select at the top right (every panel of the project): keep it as it is (recommended), or remove it because the list can find any panel?
