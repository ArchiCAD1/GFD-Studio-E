What changes

  1. 1One title row: title, an i that opens "How this page works" (the full intro text and the Project settings link, left), and the supply chain. The three-line intro and the separate Supply line go.
  2. 2Tabs and board chips share one row; the board chip row goes. When a board serves rooms, the rooms check becomes a short chip at the end of this row (full text on hover).
  3. 3A row of add buttons right above the grid, where the "Excel keys" hint is today. The phase load stays at its right; the Excel keys move behind the "?".
  4. 4ELCB / RCD (new): a 3-phase ELCB over chosen ways or for one 3-phase circuit, or a 1-phase RCBO in place of a circuit's MCB. Today ELCBs come only from "ELCB per" for the whole board.
  5. 5Spare 1-phase (20 A SP) and Spare 3-phase (32 A TP) as two buttons, no menu to open; Space asks 1-phase or 3-phase. Same commands as the ribbon's Spare menu.
  6. 6Balance phases shown here too, beside the imbalance it fixes.
  7. 7The board bar says how many ELCBs were placed by hand, so the "ELCB per" setting is not misleading.
  8. 8In the grid a hand-placed ELCB has its own cell in the ELCB column (dashed here only to mark it as new); an RCBO shows in the MCB cell.

Decisions for you

  1. A. 3-phase ELCB: 4-pole (TP&N, as the form prints today) by default, with 3-pole as a choice — or 3-pole only?
  2. B. New rows: next free way, as today (recommended) — or after the selected row, which renumbers every way below it?
  3. C. 1-phase RCBO in place of the MCB: wanted now, or only the 3-phase ELCB?
  4. D. Intro text only behind the i: OK, or keep one short line under the title?
  5. E. Ribbon keeps Add circuit / Spare as they are (recommended): both places run the same commands.
