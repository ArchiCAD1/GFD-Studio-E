SLD · equipment as a thin strip at the drawing's edge, and on the Board tab too (proposal 2026-11-15)

Today the equipment library is the second tab of the 230 px side panel: category tiles, then the open category as tiles four across. It is there only on the System diagram; on the Board tab it is gone, so nothing can be dropped on one board. Proposed: a 44 px strip on the left edge of the drawing, one symbol per category, opening a one-column list beside it. The same strip is on the Board tab, where items drop on that board's busbar or on one of its ways.
