SLD — right-click menus and edit tools (proposed)

Proposal mockup, not built. The drawing is the app's own system diagram of the demonstration project, rendered from its code. The menus use Design CAD's right-click menu: a header naming what was clicked, the ribbon's icon on each row, keys on the right, a tick for what is on, lists beside their row. A blue bar marks a new tool; every other row already works somewhere else (ribbon, Panels tree, double-click or keys), and the menu gives it a place on the drawing.

SMDB-FF · SMDB · 250 A · from MDB-1
A right-click selects what is under the pointer and opens its menu; Shift+F10 or the menu key opens it for the selection. Rows that don't apply are left off rather than greyed: Paste shows only while a board is copied (here DB-GF1), Incomer length checked only when the length is flagged. The menu stays inside the drawing. Esc or a click elsewhere closes it.
