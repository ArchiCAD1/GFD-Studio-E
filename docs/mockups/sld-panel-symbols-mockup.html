SLD panel symbols – today and proposed

Proposal, 2026-11-16; main boards changed after your feedback (section 2b); all decisions approved the same day. Screen drawings use the app's dark canvas; print drawings are black and white, as the app prints them.

1. A board drawn as an end point: today and proposed

Same 44-unit width as today and half the height, so nothing else on the SLD moves. In DEWA style the dashed panel frame stays around it (section 4). Boards drawn with their busbar and ways do not change.

2. Your seven symbols, and what Option A uses them for

Option A rule: the fill shows the level (none = final board, half = sub-main or MCC), and a double frame = main board. A cross (X) = emergency supply. A split across the middle = motor control. Letters for special panels (UPS, MC).

2b. Main boards: four options instead of all filled (your feedback; M1 chosen)

An all-filled block prints as a heavy black patch, and its edge is lost in a black-and-white print. M1 builds on your idea: the sub-main symbol (SMDB, or ESMDB for emergency) with a mark that says "main board", here a second frame.

3. Three options for our 13 board kinds

A: every kind has its own pattern and all seven of your sketches are used. It needs a legend, which the SLD and riser print on their own with the kinds drawn.

B: fewer patterns; the E reads without a legend; it adds a text mark to every emergency board and leaves the crossed sketches unused.

C: no legend needed, but it is a box with text, hard to read at drawing size and close to today's square.

4. On the SLD (DEWA style): screen with Colour by → Supply, and print

On screen, Colour by → Supply (off by default): emergency red, UPS blue, existing grey; status dots after the names.

Printed (sheet, PDF, DXF): black and white; the legend lists the board symbols drawn.

5. Combinations (Option A)

Special panels (FP, UPS, MC) take the same existing and selected marks. UPS boards show blue in the Supply view. The kind decides the symbol, so the print shows what the designer set; the Supply view shows what the network really feeds, so a DB drawn red under an EMDB is a board whose kind should be EDB.

6. One symbol everywhere (SMDB, DB, EDB, MCC)

Boards drawn with their busbar, ways and DEWA frame do not change.

7. Panels we could add as kinds (decision 5)

Decisions

  1. Symbol set: A families – chosen.
  2. Colours: Colour by → Supply, screen only, prints black – chosen.
  3. Where: everywhere at once: SLD end boards and legend, riser, feeder riser, library blocks, strip icons – chosen.
  4. Design CAD plan symbols for boards: the same patterns, tag beside – chosen.
  5. New kinds: ESMDB – chosen.
  6. Main boards (MDB, LV-MDB, LV-ACB, EMDB, LV-EMDB): M1 double frame – chosen 2026-11-16.
